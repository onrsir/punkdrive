Night Fare Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Night Fare is a free single-player driving game supported by ads. The game itself has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or other personal details.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, a Google Play certified ad network. Because Night Fare is for all ages, every ad request is marked as directed to children and to users under the age of consent, and is limited to content suitable for general audiences. Ads are not personalized, the game does not access the advertising identifier (it does not request the Android advertising ID permission or iOS tracking permission), and it does not track you across other apps or websites. To serve ads, Google may process limited technical data such as IP address, device and app information and ad interactions, as needed to deliver ads, prevent fraud and count ad views.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Data stored on your device

Game progress (missions, races, collected sigils, unlocked cars), settings, language and play time are kept only on your device and are removed when you delete the game. We do not run our own servers and do not sell personal data.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It has no accounts, chat, purchases or user-generated content, and we do not collect personal information ourselves. Ads are child-directed and non-personalized as described above. Parents can contact us at the address below with any question.
